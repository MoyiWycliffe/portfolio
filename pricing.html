01 — How Pricing Works

You pay for the work you need.

There is no fake "one website fits all" pricing. A simple five-page business website and a full web application are completely different engineering projects.

Pages

More pages mean more layouts, content structures, responsive states, testing and implementation work.

Design

A basic informational website requires less design work than a highly customised product experience.

Functionality

Authentication, payments, dashboards, databases, APIs and automation increase engineering requirements.

Responsive Design

Mobile, tablet and desktop experiences are considered as part of the build rather than an afterthought.

Time

The estimate includes development, testing, revisions and integration work where applicable.

Complexity

The final quote can change when a project requires custom functionality outside the selected configuration.
